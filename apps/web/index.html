AV-Track
Acoustic · Visual · AR

Track anything with sound + sight

Fuses near-ultrasonic sonar with on-device vision to follow a person or pet and lock a 3D marker onto them in augmented reality.

1Hold the phone in landscape
2Allow camera & microphone access
3Point at a person, cat or dog

Emits a faint near-ultrasonic sweep (18.5–21.5 kHz). Audible to some people and pets — keep the volume moderate.
